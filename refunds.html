Deposit, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This policy explains how deposits, cancellations, and refunds work for services from The Sterling Club. If your written quote or agreement says something different, your written quote or agreement controls.

Quotes

Prices on our website are estimates. Before any work starts, we give you a written quote with your price and any deposit due. Work begins only after you approve it.

Deposits

Some bookings, especially Christmas lighting, require a deposit to reserve your date. The deposit amount is shown on your written quote and is credited toward your final price.

Cancelling or rescheduling

  • 48 hours or more before your appointment: you can cancel for a full refund of your deposit, or move your deposit to a new date.
  • Less than 48 hours before your appointment, or if we cannot access the property or vehicle at the scheduled time: your deposit is non-refundable.

To cancel or reschedule, call or text (786) 488-9561 or email reserve@thesterlingclub.com. The 48-hour period is measured from when we receive your message.

If we need to cancel

If we cancel or need to reschedule, including for weather or safety reasons, you can choose a new date or receive a full refund of your deposit.

Problems with completed work

If you have a problem with work we performed, tell us within 7 days of the service date. We will inspect the issue and, if it is caused by our workmanship, fix it at no charge. Payments for completed work are generally not refunded, because we resolve problems by returning to fix them. If we are unable to fix a problem caused by our work, contact us and we will work with you on a fair resolution.

Memberships

Membership cancellation and refund terms are set out in your written membership agreement.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned promptly to the original payment method, unless we agree otherwise with you.

Your rights under law

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under Texas or federal law that cannot be waived.
