Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information The Sterling Club ("we", "us") collects through this website and when you contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are a home and auto services business based in Houston, Texas.

The short version

  • This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or social media pixels.
  • We only receive the information you choose to send us by email, phone call, or text.
  • We use it to respond to you, schedule and perform services, and keep business records.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you request a quote, call, text, or email us, you may give us your name, phone number, email address, service address or ZIP code, the service you want, your preferred date and time, how you prefer to be contacted, details about your home or vehicle (such as year, make, and model), and anything else you include in your message.

The booking form on this website does not send information to our servers. When you press the button, your own email app opens a message addressed to us, and nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.

Information that stays in your browser

The ZIP code check runs entirely in your web browser. What you enter in them is not sent to us unless you choose to include it in a quote request.

Information collected automatically

We do not use cookies, analytics tools, or tracking technologies on this website. This website is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages). GitHub may log technical information, such as your IP address, when you visit, for security and to operate its service. GitHub handles that information under its own privacy statement. The fonts on this website are stored on the website itself, so loading them does not send your information to a font provider.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and quote requests
  • To schedule, perform, and follow up on services you request
  • To send quotes, invoices, receipts, and appointment updates
  • To keep business, tax, and accounting records
  • To protect our business and customers, and to comply with the law

Calls, texts, and emails

If you give us your phone number or email address, we may contact you by call, text, or email about your request or service. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to any text to stop receiving texts. We will not send you marketing texts unless you separately agree to receive them.

When we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only:

  • With companies that help us run the business, such as our email, phone, and payment providers, only as needed to provide their services to us
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our customers, our business, or others
  • As part of a sale or transfer of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information

Sensitive information

We do not ask for sensitive personal information, such as Social Security numbers or health information, through this website. Please do not send payment card numbers by email, text, or through this website.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as we need it to provide services, keep required business and tax records, and resolve disputes. When we no longer need it, we delete it.

How we protect information

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you send us. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, and email in particular is not always encrypted.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can opt out of texts at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy law. To make a request, email reserve@thesterlingclub.com or call (786) 488-9561. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within 45 days. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

This website does not track visitors over time or across other websites, so it treats every visitor the same whether or not their browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

Children

Our services and this website are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

This policy covers only our website and business. Other websites we link to, such as GitHub, have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
