Cookie Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

This policy explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies.

We do not use cookies

This website does not set any cookies. It also does not use local storage, analytics tools, advertising trackers, social media pixels, or embedded content from other companies (such as videos or maps) that could set their own cookies.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can store in your browser. They are often used to remember settings, measure visits, or track people across websites for advertising.

Our hosting provider

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information, such as your IP address, when you visit, for security and to operate its service. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Fonts

Our fonts are stored on this website rather than loaded from a third-party font service, so displaying them does not share your information with another company.

Why there is no cookie banner

Cookie consent banners are used to ask permission before setting cookies or using trackers that are not strictly necessary. Because this website sets no cookies and uses no trackers, there is nothing to ask permission for.

If this changes

If we add analytics, advertising, or other tools that use cookies, we will update this policy before doing so and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
