Questions, answered

What areas do you serve?

[Your City] and the surrounding area. If you're not sure you're in range, send the quote form and we'll tell you.

When should I book Christmas lights?

Install dates fill up from October through early December, so book as early as you can. Platinum members get first pick of dates.

Do I need to be home?

No. For car services we need the keys and a safe, level place to work. For gutters and lights we just need access to the outside of the house.

What do I need to provide?

Nothing. We bring our own water, power, tools, parts, and supplies.

Can I combine services in one visit?

Yes. A gutter clean before your lights go up, or a detail with an oil change, saves you a trip charge. Mention it in the quote form.

How does the membership work?

Pick Sterling or Platinum and we schedule your included services through the year. Anything beyond your plan is billed at member pricing.
