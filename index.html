    Questions

    What areas do you serve?

    The Houston area. Use the ZIP check above, or send a quote request and we'll confirm whether we can travel to you.

    When should I book Christmas lights?

    As early as you can. Install dates are limited during the holiday season.

    Do you maintain the lights during the season?

    Yes. Our lighting service includes installation, maintenance through the season, and takedown after the holidays. Your written quote lists exactly what is included.

    Do I need to be home?

    Not always. When we schedule, we'll tell you what access we need, such as keys for a vehicle or access to the outside of your property.

    Do you work on businesses as well as homes?

    Yes. We serve both residential and commercial customers.

    Is a deposit required?

    Some bookings, especially Christmas lighting, need a deposit. Any deposit is shown on your written quote. Deposits are refundable if you cancel at least 48 hours before your appointment. See our deposit & cancellation policy.

    How do I pay?

    Any remaining balance is due when the job is complete. We'll confirm the payment methods we accept with your quote. Please don't send card numbers by email or through this website.

    How does the membership work?

    Membership details are confirmed in a written agreement before you join. A membership never renews or charges you without your written agreement.
